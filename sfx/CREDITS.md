# Sound credits

Every sound's licence and source is in `index.json` (`license`, `source`, `title`).

- **CC0 (public domain dedication):** OpenGameArt packs
  - Interface Sounds by Kenney: https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds
  - 51 UI sound effects: https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks
  - 100 CC0 SFX: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
  - 75 CC0 breaking, falling and hit SFX: https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx
  - 63 digital sound effects: https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc
  - Swishes Sound Pack: https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
  - Sounds added later with `scripts/sfx-fetch.mjs` come from Openverse with `license: CC0` and their own source link.

Short names like `whoosh`, `click` or `riser` are copies of CC0 sounds above (`title` in `index.json` names the original). None of these require attribution. Never add NonCommercial, ShareAlike or NoDerivatives sounds; `sfx-import.mjs` refuses them.
